// ORIGIN

MY STORY

My professional journey in website development began in October 2025. Since then, I have dedicated myself entirely to the pursuit of modern frontend excellence. I approach the DOM not merely as markup, but as a rigid structural foundation requiring absolute discipline.

I built my resilience by diving directly into single-page bug-fixing and architectural overhauls on freelance networks, diagnosing rendering bottlenecks and logic faults under strict deadlines. That crucible forged my philosophy: web interfaces must possess both a luxurious aesthetic and an unbreakable underlying logic. This standard is evident in the offline, SVG-driven mathematical precision of web games like Teen Tani, and the seamless local dependencies I orchestrate within custom 3D digital book editors.

My eye for motion aesthetics predates my code. Years of producing, color-grading, and rendering 4K gaming montages taught me that timing and visual hierarchy are everything. Today, I translate that kinetic energy from video frames into scroll-driven animations, leveraging Vite and React Three Fiber to ensure the browser responds to the user with the same immediacy and impact.
