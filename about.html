// ORIGIN

MY STORY

My professional journey in website development began in October 2025. Since then, I have dedicated myself entirely to the pursuit of modern frontend excellence. I started by mastering the fundamental building blocks of the web—HTML and CSS—focusing heavily on how layout, spacing, and typography combine to create an impactful digital presence.

As my skills progressed, I seamlessly transitioned into JavaScript, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. My core focus has always remained the same: pushing the boundaries of design quality, responsive behavior, and user interaction. I don't just write code; I engineer client-ready digital environments that feel deliberate, expensive, and technically sound.
